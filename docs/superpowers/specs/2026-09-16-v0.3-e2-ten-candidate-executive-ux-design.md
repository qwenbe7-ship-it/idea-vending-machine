# v0.3 E2 — Ten-Candidate Reality Evaluation + Executive UX + Browser E2E

Date: 2026-09-16
Status: Design baseline for user review
Base: `main @ b81d2da3b2e0ad36ade216babdf1f0c0b5352894`

## 1. Goal

Finish the user-facing v0.3 product by changing Candidate Forge from four candidate families to **exactly ten structurally distinct business candidates**, independently evaluating every candidate with evidence-aware and deliberately skeptical criteria, and exposing the full flow through an Executive UX that is verified in a real browser.

The final product flow is:

`RAW IDEA → RESEARCH → EVIDENCE GRAPH → ASSUMPTION DESTRUCTION → REFRAMING → MECHANISM TRANSFER → EXACTLY 10 CANDIDATES → COLLISION RESEARCH → REALITY EVALUATION OF ALL 10 → DETERMINISTIC OFFICIAL DECISION → EXECUTIVE UX → HUMAN APPROVAL → SPEC/DESIGN/PLAN`

The core rule remains:

> NO GREEN WITHOUT EVIDENCE.

A provider may create hypotheses and critiques, but it cannot create trusted IDs, official verdicts, confidence, selected concepts, or human approval.

## 2. Scope decomposition

This architectural change will be implemented in two sequential PRs after this design is approved.

### E2A — Ten-Candidate Reality Evaluation

Changes the backend contracts and runtime:

- candidate family vocabulary grows from 4 to 10,
- runtime must generate exactly one candidate for every canonical family,
- no family may be silently marked non-applicable,
- every candidate is independently critiqued on all ten existing evaluation dimensions,
- every candidate receives a deterministic Reality Verdict,
- report payload exposes all ten reality assessments,
- official `GO / MODIFY / HOLD / KILL` semantics remain separate from per-candidate Reality Verdicts.

### E2B — Executive UX + Human Approval + Browser E2E

Changes the user-facing flow:

- `/api/evolve` becomes the primary front-door action,
- Executive Decision appears before detail,
- ten candidate Reality Cards are rendered,
- evidence and detailed analysis are expandable,
- development package generation is blocked until explicit human approval,
- GO/MODIFY approval uses the already-validated v0.2 handoff,
- HOLD/KILL and runtime-incomplete states cannot generate a development package,
- real Chromium E2E becomes part of the final Production Gate.

No SaaS accounts, billing, multi-tenancy, database, arbitrary code execution, or client-controlled provider configuration are introduced.

## 3. Canonical ten candidate families

The runtime must create **exactly one trusted candidate per family**. All ten families are mandatory. A weak family is still generated and then honestly rejected or held by the evaluator rather than hidden.

1. `adjacent_innovation`
   - Preserve the strongest part of the raw idea while making the smallest structural change that materially increases buyer value.

2. `category_shift`
   - Change the problem definition, purchasing category, or category in which the buyer evaluates alternatives.

3. `zero_based_reinvention`
   - Ignore the proposed solution form and redesign from the underlying job and constraints.

4. `axion_automation`
   - Maximize E2E automation, low recurring human labor, verifiable output, operational reliability, and capital efficiency.

5. `prevention_shift_left`
   - Move value from detecting or repairing a problem after the fact to preventing it before cost/risk materializes.

6. `outcome_execution`
   - Move from advice/tooling to executing the measurable result or workflow outcome for the customer.

7. `workflow_infrastructure`
   - Turn a point feature into reusable workflow infrastructure across repeated jobs, teams, or process stages.

8. `data_decision_asset`
   - Convert transient work into structured proprietary data, decision signals, benchmarks, or reusable decision assets.

9. `buyer_revenue_inversion`
   - Reconsider user vs buyer vs beneficiary vs payer, and redesign distribution or value capture around the party with the strongest economic incentive.

10. `compounding_asset`
    - Convert one-time service value into a compounding asset such as reusable process IP, templates, evidence, normalized data, benchmark history, or automation components without creating a SaaS business by default.

### Family invariants

- exactly 10 candidates,
- exactly 10 unique families,
- no duplicate candidate IDs,
- no `non_applicable_families` escape hatch,
- each candidate still needs explicit unknowns or validation questions,
- every candidate must contain a complete causal value-creation chain,
- cosmetic renaming does not satisfy diversity.

## 4. Candidate diversity

A ten-candidate result is not useful if the provider creates ten feature variants.

The existing diversity dimensions remain:

- primary buyer,
- problem reframe,
- workflow after,
- value-creation chain,
- value-capture model,
- transformations used,
- mechanism transfers.

E2A strengthens admission with the following deterministic conditions:

1. every canonical family appears exactly once,
2. every candidate differs materially from at least four other candidates on at least three diversity dimensions,
3. no two candidates may have identical canonicalized values for `problem_reframe + workflow_after + value_capture_model`,
4. all candidates must pass causal-chain validation,
5. every unknown decision-critical fact becomes a Collision Research request rather than an invented fact.

If the ten-candidate set does not pass, runtime status is `incomplete`; it does not proceed to an official business verdict.

## 5. Reality Evaluation of every candidate

Every candidate is independently evaluated after Collision Research. The evaluator receives validated candidate objects and Evidence Graph material; it does not receive generator preferences, rankings, hidden reasoning, or a suggested winner.

The ten existing evaluation dimensions remain canonical:

1. `problem_evidence`
2. `buyer_economics`
3. `structural_differentiation`
4. `competitive_collision`
5. `automation_leverage`
6. `technical_feasibility`
7. `operational_reliability`
8. `regulatory_security_data_risk`
9. `distribution_feasibility`
10. `defensibility_compounding`

Each dimension uses the closed status vocabulary:

- `strong`
- `mixed`
- `weak`
- `unknown`

Each critique must also expose:

- strongest reason for,
- strongest reason against,
- unacceptable conditions,
- cheapest next validation,
- material unknowns,
- evidence-backed blockers,
- advisory recommendation.

Provider recommendation remains advisory only.

## 6. Candidate Reality Verdict

E2A introduces a deterministic per-candidate Reality Verdict that is separate from the project-level official decision.

Reality Verdict vocabulary:

- `ADVANCE`
- `REVISE`
- `HOLD`
- `REJECT`

### ADVANCE

A candidate may be `ADVANCE` only if:

- it is eligible under existing deterministic hard gates,
- decision-critical evidence is ready,
- no unresolved material unknown remains,
- no hard blocker remains,
- no critical evaluation dimension is `weak` or `unknown`,
- positive confidence is not low.

### REVISE

Use when the candidate has meaningful value but one or more material, resolvable weaknesses prevent direct advancement. Typical cases include a weak buyer/value-capture design, a distribution weakness, a technical dependency that can be redesigned, or a collision that requires a structural modification.

### HOLD

Use when reality cannot yet be determined because material evidence is missing, stale, contradictory, or a decision-critical unknown remains. Missing evidence is never converted to `REJECT`.

### REJECT

Use only when there is evidence-backed failure such as an unresolvable hard blocker, economic non-viability, fatal regulatory/technical constraints, or a candidate that remains structurally inferior after collision research.

### No fake precision

E2 does not create a weighted 0–100 business-success score. The UI may summarize dimension counts, but must not imply a mathematically precise probability of business success.

## 7. Relationship between Reality Verdict and official decision

Candidate Reality Verdict answers:

> “How realistic is this candidate on its own?”

Official decision answers:

> “What should happen to the original idea after comparing the original baseline and all ten candidates?”

Official semantics remain:

- `GO`: original idea remains the development direction.
- `MODIFY`: one evolved candidate is the development direction.
- `HOLD`: no safe direction can yet be selected because decision-critical uncertainty remains.
- `KILL`: baseline and all viable evolved alternatives have evidence-backed decisive failure.

A candidate being `ADVANCE` does not automatically cause `MODIFY`. Existing strict-dominance and confidence rules remain authoritative.

If several evolved candidates qualify but no single candidate strictly dominates the relevant alternatives, official decision remains `HOLD` rather than creating a fake winner.

## 8. Runtime/report contract changes

A completed evolution response must expose:

- runtime record,
- Evolution State,
- Evidence Graph,
- exactly 10 candidates,
- critiques for baseline + all 10 candidates,
- candidate reality assessments for all 10 candidates,
- official deterministic decision result,
- complete report data.

The report adds `candidate_reality_assessments` with one record per candidate:

- candidate_id,
- family,
- name,
- one_sentence_concept,
- reality_verdict,
- confidence,
- strongest_reason_for,
- strongest_reason_against,
- dimension_statuses,
- hard_or_material_blockers,
- material_unknowns,
- cheapest_next_validation,
- evidence_claim_ids.

All IDs and official fields remain server-owned.

## 9. Executive UX information architecture

The final web experience must be decision-first, not document-first.

### Screen 1 — Input

Primary action:

`아이디어 진화시키기`

The user submits only the idea text.

### Screen 2 — Runtime progress

Show truthful stages, based on runtime stage events rather than simulated timers:

- 아이디어 구조 파악
- 글로벌 시장/문제 조사
- 반대 근거 조사
- 숨은 전제 분석
- 관점 재구성
- 타 산업 메커니즘 이전
- 10개 사업 후보 생성
- 경쟁/선행사례 충돌검사
- 10개 후보 독립 현실평가
- 최종 경영판단
- 대표이사 보고서 구성

If runtime becomes incomplete or failed, progress stops and the UI shows the operational failure without inventing `HOLD` or `KILL`.

### Screen 3 — Executive Decision

Above the fold:

- official `GO / MODIFY / HOLD / KILL`,
- confidence,
- one-sentence thesis,
- original → evolved delta,
- key market snapshot,
- strongest reasons for (3),
- strongest reasons against (3),
- critical unknowns,
- cheapest next validation.

### Screen 4 — Ten Candidate Reality Cards

Render all 10 candidates. A card shows:

- family and candidate name,
- one-sentence concept,
- Reality Verdict,
- primary buyer,
- value capture model,
- strongest reason for,
- strongest reason against,
- cheapest next validation,
- compact dimension summary.

Expanding a card shows all 10 evaluation dimensions, blockers, material unknowns, evidence references, workflow before/after, causal value chain, risks, dependencies, and validation questions.

The UI must not hide REJECT candidates; rejected ideas are valuable evidence of explored alternatives.

### Screen 5 — Evidence / Detailed report

Expandable sections follow the canonical report order and show source metadata for material claims.

### Screen 6 — Human approval / development handoff

- `GO` or `MODIFY`: show `이 방향 승인`.
- `HOLD`: disable development handoff and show required validation.
- `KILL`: disable development handoff and show evidence-backed reasons.
- `incomplete/failed`: disable business-decision and development actions.

The user cannot send a client-side `decision`, `confidence`, `selected_candidate_id`, or `human_decision` to override trusted state.

## 10. Server-side assessment store and approval API

E2B adds a minimal in-memory assessment store. This is intentionally not a database or SaaS account system.

### Store record

Key: trusted `runtime_id`.

Value contains validated server-side:

- evolution result,
- trusted state,
- candidates,
- report,
- creation timestamp,
- approval status.

### API changes

`POST /api/evolve`

Input:

```json
{"idea": "..."}
```

Completed responses include a trusted `runtime_id` and store the validated result server-side.

`POST /api/evolve/approve`

Input:

```json
{"runtime_id": "runtime_..."}
```

Rules:

1. lookup must resolve a server-owned assessment,
2. runtime must be completed,
3. report/state/evidence must still validate,
4. official decision must be `GO` or `MODIFY`,
5. server sets `human_decision = proceed`,
6. existing `generate_approved_development_package()` creates `spec.md / design.md / plan.md`,
7. replay is deterministic and may return the existing package rather than generate divergent output.

The browser cannot choose or override a candidate during approval. If the user wants another direction, they refine the idea and run a new assessment rather than mutating an official result.

### Store limitations

- memory only,
- process restart loses assessments,
- bounded capacity and expiry required to avoid unbounded memory growth,
- no cross-device persistence.

These limitations are explicit and acceptable for v0.3.

## 11. Frontend security

Existing rules remain:

- no `innerHTML`,
- render untrusted text via `textContent` / DOM nodes,
- existing CSP remains,
- browser cannot provide provider configuration,
- evidence URLs are validated server-side before rendering,
- external links use safe `rel` values,
- no secrets, prompts, provider raw payloads, or authorization headers are exposed in the DOM.

## 12. Browser E2E

E2B introduces a **test-only Playwright dependency**. Production runtime remains Python 3.11 stdlib plus HTML/CSS/JS.

Preferred implementation: Python Playwright with pinned test dependency and Chromium in GitHub Actions.

### Deterministic Browser E2E

Browser tests use an injected deterministic `evolve_runner`; paid/live provider calls are not made in normal CI.

Required browser scenarios:

1. successful MODIFY path
   - enter idea,
   - start evolve,
   - result shows official decision,
   - exactly 10 Reality Cards render,
   - all ten unique families are visible,
   - expand at least one card and verify ten dimensions,
   - approve direction,
   - `spec.md / design.md / plan.md` render.

2. successful GO path
   - approval uses original idea semantics.

3. HOLD path
   - validation guidance is visible,
   - approval/development handoff disabled.

4. KILL path
   - evidence-backed reasons visible,
   - approval/development handoff disabled.

5. runtime incomplete path
   - operational failure shown,
   - no business verdict manufactured,
   - approval disabled.

6. provider-not-configured path
   - configuration error shown without secrets.

7. frontend security
   - malicious text is rendered as text rather than executable markup.

### CI structure

Production Gate becomes two layers:

1. existing deterministic compile/security/unit/integration gate,
2. deterministic Chromium browser E2E gate.

Live-provider smoke remains opt-in and separate. Browser GREEN does not mean LIVE PROVIDER GREEN.

## 13. Compatibility

- `/api/analyze` semantics remain unchanged.
- `/api/package` remains available for legacy v0.2 compatibility but is no longer the primary final UX path.
- `/api/evolve` remains server-controlled.
- v0.2 Development Package generator is reused, not rewritten.
- existing GO/MODIFY/HOLD/KILL and human-approval contracts remain authoritative.

## 14. Failure handling

- provider timeout/auth/rate limit/schema failure → runtime `incomplete`, official decision unset,
- internal contract violation → runtime `failed`, official decision unset,
- insufficient evidence after successful research → completed business `HOLD`,
- evidence-backed fatal failure across baseline and alternatives → completed business `KILL`,
- candidate-set diversity/family admission failure → runtime `incomplete`, not a business verdict,
- browser/network failure → UX operational error, never a business decision.

## 15. Acceptance criteria

E2 is complete only when all of the following are demonstrated:

1. exactly 10 canonical candidate families are generated in a successful run,
2. each family appears exactly once,
3. all candidates pass trusted-ID and causal-chain validation,
4. all 10 candidates receive complete ten-dimension critiques,
5. all 10 receive deterministic Reality Verdicts,
6. REJECT candidates remain visible to the user,
7. official decision remains separate from per-candidate Reality Verdicts,
8. `GO/MODIFY/HOLD/KILL` regression behavior remains correct,
9. Executive UX renders the decision first,
10. exactly 10 Reality Cards render in a successful browser flow,
11. Human approval is required before package generation,
12. HOLD/KILL/incomplete/failed cannot produce a development package,
13. browser cannot inject provider or official-decision fields,
14. legacy `/api/analyze` and `/api/package` regressions pass,
15. full unit/integration suite passes,
16. real Chromium E2E suite passes,
17. Production Gate ends in `GREEN WITH EVIDENCE`,
18. live-provider readiness is not claimed unless the separate opt-in live smoke succeeds.
