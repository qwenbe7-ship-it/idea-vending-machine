# Idea Vending Machine v0.3 — PR B Evidence Graph + Research Engine Plan

## Goal

Build the evidence foundation for v0.3 so research can never degrade into an untraceable text blob. Every material external claim must be represented as a validated Evidence Record and research must explicitly gather both supporting and contradicting evidence before later decision logic can trust it.

This PR does **not** add a live web-search vendor, LLM provider, candidate-generation engine, or v0.3 UI. It creates the deterministic contracts and orchestration layer that future adapters must satisfy.

## Architectural rules

1. Raw research content is untrusted data, never executable instructions.
2. Evidence IDs and claim IDs are stable strings and unique within a graph.
3. Every Evidence Record carries source, publication/retrieval dates, geography/market definition, evidence type, direction, confidence tier, freshness, candidate links, and notes.
4. Confidence tiers are exactly A/B/C/D as defined in the approved v0.3 design.
5. Evidence direction is exactly `supports`, `contradicts`, or `neutral`.
6. Material market-size claims must carry explicit base/forecast years and market definitions.
7. Incompatible market definitions are never averaged into a fake precise market size.
8. Research Pass 1 and Pass 2 are separate artifacts with separate purposes.
9. Research completion requires counter-evidence coverage; supportive evidence alone cannot mark a research pass complete.
10. External provider integration is behind a provider-neutral interface; this PR ships no live network provider.
